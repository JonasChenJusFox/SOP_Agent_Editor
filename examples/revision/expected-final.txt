I want to understand how distributed systems recover when their inputs are unreliable.

With three classmates, I built a campus shuttle tracker. I wrote the parser that turned inconsistent arrival messages into a single feed and built the replay tool to investigate failures using saved messages. Our team tested it with 20 saved messages; we did not measure campus-wide shuttle delays.

Reviewing those errors made me interested in how software can stay useful when its inputs are unreliable. The replay tool let me revisit failures using the messages that caused them. I now want to reason more systematically about recovery.

Northbridge Institute's Distributed Systems Studio is a fit for that question: its work on failure traces and recovery under unreliable inputs connects directly to the problems I encountered. I want to return to the assumptions we made in that tracker and learn how to test them.
